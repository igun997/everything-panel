```
everything-panel puts every server panel you run in one calm, glass-smooth app. Easypanel is
supported today, and the app is built so more providers can follow.

SEE EVERYTHING AT A GLANCE
- Connect several servers and watch them side by side
- Live CPU, memory and disk for each host
- How many services are running, and which ones stopped

FIND AND CONTROL SERVICES FAST
- Services grouped by project; fold and unfold groups
- Search and filters stay pinned while you scroll
- Start, stop, restart and deploy apps and stacks, with a confirmation before anything disruptive
- Read recent builds, deployments and their logs

STAY INFORMED
- Service-down alerts while the app is open

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- No account needed. Your servers and tokens stay on your device, in the Android keystore

- Free, with no ads and no tracking

MADE FOR PHONES AND TABLETS
- A liquid-glass design with smooth, spring-driven motion
- A floating bar on phones and a side rail on tablets
- Dark and light themes
```

## Before you submit

- A privacy policy URL is required because the app can sign in with Google and store data in your
  Firestore documents. Say plainly: servers and tokens stay on the device; with sync on, they are stored
  encrypted with a key only the user holds; no analytics, no ads.
- Data safety form: data is collected only if the user enables sync (encrypted, user-controlled,
  deletable). Sign-in is optional.
- Upload `everything-panel-vX.Y.Z.aab` from the GitHub release. It is signed with the upload key kept in
  the repository secrets; enrol in Play App Signing when asked.
- Feature graphic: `store/graphics/feature-graphic.png` (1024x500). Icon: `store/graphics/icon-512.png`.
- Screenshots: `store/screenshots/phone` (1080x1920), `tablet7` (1200x1920), `tablet10` (2560x1600).
